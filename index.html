What We Deliver

Four things that change
how you operate.

01

A single version of the truth.

Different clubs, different tracking vendors, different data formats. We standardise everything into one clean, coherent framework — so that when you sit down with your data, you are not managing discrepancies. You are reading reality.

02

Independence you can trust.

We are not embedded in any club. We carry no internal loyalties, no political exposure, no conflict of interest. Our role is to give you the honest picture — including the parts that are inconvenient — because that is the only kind of analysis that is actually useful.

03

Complexity made legible.

Performance science is inherently complex. The way you communicate it should not be. We adapt the depth and language of our outputs to the person receiving them — whether that is a performance director, a sporting director, or a board member making a significant investment decision.

04

From data to action.

A well-produced report that sits unread is worth nothing. We work backwards from the decision you need to make, and structure the analysis so that the path forward is visible. Not probable. Not suggested. Clear.

On Honesty

We do not sell certainty.
We build the conditions for it.

There is a version of data consultancy that sells confidence — dashboards that look authoritative, numbers that appear precise, conclusions that arrive gift-wrapped. We are not that.

Good analysis starts with clean data — collected rigorously, verified at source, and honest about its own limitations. It continues with interpretation that respects context: the tactical system, the fixture calendar, the player history. And it ends with a clear line to the decision at hand — not a document that requires a separate expert to decode.

That process is unglamorous. It is also the only thing that works.

Scientific Leadership

Rooted in elite experience.

Our unit is led by Cristian Osgnach — a researcher whose work on metabolic power and physical load in football is referenced across the international sports science community, and who has spent over a decade applying that knowledge at the highest levels of the game, including Serie A and the Italian National Team.

This is not a technology company that has learned football. It is a team of people who understand elite performance from the inside, and who have built the methodological framework to make that knowledge transferable, scalable, and institutionally useful.
